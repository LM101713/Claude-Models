## Title

Gift Slide: Card & Cash Gift Box with a Click-Glide-Stop Lid (2 parts, no supports)

## Summary (first lines)

Push the dish: *click*, the lid glides, *stop*, and there's the gift card. Gift Slide is a slim, two-piece box for any standard gift card plus a few folded notes. A hidden printed spring gives the lid a crisp click shut and a firm stop open. It holds itself closed in a pocket or stocking, and the lid can't slide off and get lost.

* 2 parts, no screws, no springs, no glue: the lid snaps in once and stays captive
* No supports, prints flat, ≈ 30 min and ≈ 25 g of PLA on an A1 mini
* Holds a standard card (85.6 × 54 mm) + up to 4 notes folded in quarters
* A finger hole underneath tips the card up; **FOR YOU** is waiting under it
* Fit coupon (≈ 28 min) with Standard / Loose / Snug lids, so it works on your printer the first time
* Optional gold star inlay (AMS) and two-colour builds without an AMS

## Print profile notes

0.20 mm Standard, PLA, textured PEI, no supports, no brim. Print the lid thumb-dish **up**. Print `GS05_fit_coupon_first.3mf` once per printer and choose your lid from the coupon.

## How to use

1. Slide the lid into the open end of the tray, dish up, until it snaps and then clicks shut.
2. Open, drop the card in (notes folded in quarters underneath), close.
3. To open: thumb in the dish, push. To take the card out: push up through the hole underneath.
4. To take the lid off completely, pull firmly past the stop.
