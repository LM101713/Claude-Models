# Build plates (one engine)

Generated by `tools/plates.py` for the **Bambu Lab H2C**, bed 325 x 320 mm in single-nozzle mode (dual-nozzle mode is 300 x 320; plates marked * do not fit it). One 3MF per plate in `stl/plates/`, parts placed and ready to slice with your own printer, filament and process profiles; layout drawings in `renders/plates/`. Times/grams are PrusaSlicer estimates with the production settings (`docs/PRINT_REPORT.md`).

**Print order:** C1 first (fits), then M1-M6 (motion test), then F1-F9. Material: coupons and motion test in PLA (or PETG for the printed pins/shafts); production parts in ASA. The A1 Mini (180 mm) can print C1's parts individually and plates M1-M3 and F9; everything else needs the H2C/H2S.
