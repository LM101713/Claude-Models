# Production parts plan: every part of one engine, and how we get 50 of it

Written 2026-11-25 for the 50-engine order. One line per part: what it is, how it is made, how
many, where it comes from, and whether engine #1 has proven it yet.

**Nothing here is ordered for 50 yet.** The rule: order production parts only after engine #1 has
passed its tests, the design is frozen, and the customer has confirmed and paid a deposit.

Status key: **proven** = works on engine #1 · **testing** = being tried on engine #1 now ·
**not yet** = not made or tried yet.

Where the numbers come from: part list and print hours from `docs/BOM.md` (generated by
`tools/bom.py`); changes made since then are listed in section 7.

## 7. Changed since `docs/BOM.md` was generated

`tools/bom.py` still lists some old items. This plan is the current one:

- **No LED strip, no level shifter** (LEDs removed, D77). The BOM still lists E4 and E12.
- **Regulator is the D24V25F5** (bought), not the D24V22F5.
- **Power rocker is 2-pin and unlit**, not illuminated.
- **Controller is the custom PCB** (D90), not a perfboard (E13).
- **Name plate is not numbered** (your call), and it is brass on the walnut base.
- **Crankpin has no offset journal** (straight pin, split angle 0).
- **Insert hole is 4.05 mm** (T2, D87), and pistons get reamed and glue-locked (D91).
