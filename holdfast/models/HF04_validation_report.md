Roller ≈ 7.5 g printed (estimate); gravity pulls it down the ramp with 70 mN, with no pivot friction to overcome. Base ≈ 44 g.

Elastic Hertz p₀ is a point-contact estimate. Local yield in the roller rim starts near p₀ ≈ 1.6 σy (≈ 80-110 MPa for printed PLA / PETG), so on a hard steel or aluminium handle a small, contained contact mark can form above about 1 kg. On wood and plastic handles p₀ stays below that up to the 2 kg rating. A mark does not change the wedge angle, so it does not change whether the grip holds: the roller just settles a fraction of a millimetre lower. The rating is 2 kg per holder; heavier tools need the physical test in PHYSICAL_TESTING.md.
