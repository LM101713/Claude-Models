**Summary (first lines shown):**
Push the handle in and up, let go: it locks. Lift it: it lets go. Two rollers on 16° ramps
wedge the handle with its own weight, so heavy tools are held harder. One size fits 19–32 mm
brooms, mops and garden tools. Three parts, no supports, no springs, two screws.

**Description:**

- **How it works.** Each orange roller sits on a ramp leaning 16° from vertical. When the tool
  pulls down, the rollers roll down into a narrowing gap and clamp the handle. When you lift,
  they roll up and let go. Gravity resets them with a clack.
- **One size, many tools.** The rollers climb their ramps to suit the handle: 19 mm light mop
  poles up to 32 mm rake handles.
- **No springs to wear out.** No flexures, no press fits, no glue. The rollers drop in through a
  keyhole and are held captive, so it comes apart again without tools.
- **Print a few.** Most closets need 3–6. Space them 120–150 mm apart.

**Print:** PLA indoors, PETG for garages. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls on the base, no supports, files
already oriented. Print the 10-minute fit coupon with one roller first. If it is tight, use the
Loose rollers.

**Use:** push in and up, let go · lift and pull out.

**Rated:** 2 kg per holder. For chrome or gloss handles, print the Grip rollers.

**Hardware:** 2 × countersunk wood screws, 3.5–4 mm (#6–#8), ≥ 30 mm, plus plugs to suit the wall.
