## Description

A snowflake that spins inside a ring that spins inside another ring, printed in one piece.

- **Print-in-place**: comes off the bed assembled. Twist each ring once to free it.
- **No supports, no brim, no hardware.** Every overhang is 45°.
- **6 snowflakes, one frame**: Classic, Fern, Stellar, Plate, Star, Crystal. Print the whole
  set on one plate.
- **1 to 3 colours**: the outer ring, middle ring and snowflake are separate parts, so
  assigning AMS colours takes three clicks. Looks good in a single silk colour too.
- **71 mm across, 7 mm thick**, about 25 g. Sized for a tree branch, a gift tag or a desk.

### Printing
0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15 % infill, PLA or PETG. Leave elephant-foot compensation on.
Rings fused? Calibrate flow or scale to 101–102 %.

### Ideas
Hang them on the tree, tie one to a present as a reusable tag, or keep one on your desk as a
fidget. Post your colour combos as a make.
